# Responsive quick controls

When the window is at most 241 pixels high and at least 360 pixels wide,
the quick controls appear in a horizontal row to the left of the window controls.
The visibility toggle is on the left, followed by grayscale, posterize,
fewer values, the value count, more values, and the warning buttons.
The controls slide right to hide and left to reappear.

Otherwise, the original vertical layout remains available, including its
scrolling behavior for very small windows.
